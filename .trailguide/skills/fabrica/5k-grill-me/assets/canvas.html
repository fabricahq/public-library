5K Grill Me

5K Grill Me is a skill by Fabrica that brings Matt Pocock’s grill-me workflow to a visual canvas for exploring questions and recording your answers.

The design-tree approach is inspired by Fred Brooks’ The Design of Design.

Matt Pocock’s grill-me skill is shared under the MIT License.
